VALSYNC / PATCH 13.06

Diagnostic Android build

Install this debug build alongside your regular VALSYNC app to copy the field names Riot returns for a recent match and contracts. It does not copy account details or response values.

Download APK · 129 MB
  1. Open the downloaded APK on your Android phone and install it.
  2. Sign in to Riot in the debug app.
  3. Open Settings → About and tap Version seven times.
  4. Tap Patch 13.06 fields, then paste the copied text into our conversation.
